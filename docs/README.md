# Revised book chapters

Implementation of `book-style-implementation-plan.docx`, version 1.0.

The 14 Markdown files at the top level are the revised manuscript, in the original 00–13 order and with the original filenames. All chapters have completed revision and the final editorial checks. No decision about meaning or scope remains open from this revision.

The revision follows the calm, practical voice of Chapters 7, 9 and 11. Earlier explanations are more direct, recurring sections follow a common order, and British English and list conventions are aligned. The introduction teaches the method; the chapters apply it; the conclusion combines it.

The chapter body is approximately 50,071 words, compared with 58,153 in the source, a reduction of 13.9 percent. These counts include headings, exercises, lists and tables and exclude endnotes. Length was a diagnostic, not an editing target.
